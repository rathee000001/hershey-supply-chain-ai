Key Features of Common Carriers

e Public Service: They are available to any person or business, unlike private or
contract carriers that serve specific clients.

e Regulations: Must have an MC (Motor Carrier) number and DOT (Department of
Transportation) number to operate legally, often requiring at least $750k in liability
insurance.

e Operation Method: They frequently use a "hub-and-spoke" model, where freight is
moved between regional hubs, increasing handling but allowing for cost-effective
