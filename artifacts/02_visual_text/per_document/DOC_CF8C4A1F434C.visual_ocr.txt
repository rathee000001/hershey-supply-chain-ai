Q: What is the difference between a fun size Hershey bar and a snack size Hershey bar?
Ronnie55

Originally posted on HERSHEY'S Milk Chocolate Snack Size Candy Bars
1 Answer ANSWER THIS QUESTION
A: Thanks for your question! The difference between a Fun Size and a Snack Size HERSHEY'S Milk Chocolate Bar mainly comes down to the size of the candy. Fun Size
bars are smaller and typically used for party favors, Halloween treats, or quick bites — they’re perfect for a small taste of chocolate. On the other hand, Snack Size bars
are slightly larger and offer a bit more indulgence, making them ideal for a satisfying treat on the go. Both are delicious, but if you're looking for a little more chocolate
in each bite, Snack Size is the way to go!
