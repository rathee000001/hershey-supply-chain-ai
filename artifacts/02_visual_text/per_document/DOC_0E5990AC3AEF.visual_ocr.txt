Belize Sugar Industries Limited (BSI) was founded in 1963 and has been
producing quality cane sugar products since 1967. It owns the only sugarcane
mill in northern Belize and produces plantation white sugar, bulk (raw) brown
Erg sugar, standard demerara and molasses from sugarcane. BSI has an annual
BELLIES Gal milling capacity of 1.3 million tons of sugarcane. It farms sugarcane and receives
cane from more than 5,000 independent Belizean farmers. BSI also generates
renewable energy from sugarcane fiber, or bagasse, in its co-generation plant,
which powers the sugar-making operations and exports homegrown electricity to
Belize’s public power grid, providing approximately 15% of the nation’s demand.
